Sock Map

Knit a sock from the top down, one part at a time.

Everything you need before the first stitch. Gather the materials, measure one foot, and work out how many stitches to cast on.

Yarn

Sock-weight (fingering) wool with about 25% nylon. The nylon stops the heel and toe from wearing through. Expect around 400 m per 100 g.

For a first pair, pick a smooth, light, solid colour so you can see every stitch. The label lists the fibre content and the needle size to use.

Needles

A set of five double-pointed needles, or one long circular needle for the magic loop method. Sizes 2 to 3 mm suit most sock yarn.

Personally, I rather prefer 5 needles. So for this tutorial we will use them.

Wood and bamboo hold stitches better than metal, which helps while you learn.

Small kit

A stitch marker for the start of the round, a tapestry needle for the ends, and a tape measure.

Skills

Cast on, knit, purl, 1x1 or 2x2 ribbing, German short rows and binding off. Knit a gauge swatch of about 12 by 12 cm first, then count the stitches across the middle 10 cm. Without it, the sock will not fit.

Personally, I only need the width gauge. You can adjust the length as you go, but it is important that the width fits.

Measure

Foot circumference at the widest point, and foot length from the back of the heel to the tip of the longest toe.
